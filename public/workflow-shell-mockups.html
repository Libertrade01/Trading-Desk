Workflow shell previews

Ledger-frame mockups for the workflow pages.

These are desktop-only shell concepts for Check-in / Session plan / Close loop. The internal UI is kept close to your current app, while the outer frame explores different clipboard / ledger feels.

Recommended starting point: Option 2 or Option 4. They feel premium and tactile without becoming too thematic or interfering with readability.
