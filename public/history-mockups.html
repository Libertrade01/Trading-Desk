Trade Desk · History

History page mockups

Five design directions addressing tall cards, low density, small P&L/readiness, and missing filters. Dark Libertrade theme with saltire blue (#0065bd) and glass panels.

Design rationale

Design 1Compact table — spreadsheet density with sortable columns and filter chips for fast scanning.
Design 2Timeline rail — vertical chronology with P&L magnitude bars and recovery nodes on the left rail.
Design 3Card grid — 2-column tiles with hero P&L and readiness rings for at-a-glance pattern spotting.
Design 4Calendar split — heat-map month picker filters the session list by selected week.
Design 5Dashboard + dense rows — KPI strip plus single-line rows grouped by week for maximum density.
Design 1Compact Table

Replace tall cards with a sortable data table. P&L and readiness get dedicated columns at readable size; filters sit above the grid.
