Session plan · Risk

Risk section design directions.

Five ways to present today’s risk limits and rails, using the same premium glass language as the rest of the daily workflow. Copy options for the section and fields are listed first.

Section title + description options

Current Risk parameters — Pre-committing to limits before you're emotional about them.
Option A Risk limits — Lock the numbers before the open.
Option B Risk rails — Size, loss limit, and stop rules for today.
Option C Capital rules — What you can lose, how big you can go, and when you stop.
Option D Risk plan — Commit to limits while you’re still calm.
Option E Guardrails — Pre-commit size, loss, and stop conditions.

Field label options

DD from HWM Drawdown from peak · Account drawdown % · Distance from high water
Expected volatility Session volatility · Expected range · Volatility regime
Max daily loss Daily loss limit · Max loss today · Hard stop for the day
Max trades Trade cap · Max trades today · Trade budget
Position size Size per trade · Standard size · Risk unit
Stop trading at Session end rule · Kill switch · When you stop
Risk rails Broker confirmations · Rails set · Pre-flight checks
