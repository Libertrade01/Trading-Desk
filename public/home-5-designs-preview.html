Five fresh home directions

These mockups fix what felt wrong in Terminal B and the generic previews: cramped mono grids, identical card stacks, 9px labels, and dense 3-column stat walls that all read as the same AI dashboard.

  • Generous space — 24–32px section padding, 700–800px tall mocks, nothing squished
  • One hero moment — each direction leads with a single focal point, not a wall of widgets
  • Distinct personality — cinematic, editorial, ambient, poster, command — not five shades of fintech dark mode
